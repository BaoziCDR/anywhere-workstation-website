常见问题FAQ

你可能想知道Things you might ask

AnyWhere WorkStation 是什么？What is AnyWhere WorkStation?

一款 Mac 应用。它在你的 Mac 里为 Claude 准备一个独立、私密的工作空间，Claude 可以在里面放心地写代码、查资料、跑任务。A Mac app that gives Claude a private workspace of its own inside your Mac, where it can code, research and run tasks with peace of mind.

为什么不直接在 Mac 上用 Claude？Why not just run Claude on my Mac?

Claude 工作时会执行命令、安装工具、改动文件。放进独立空间后，这些操作都发生在里面，不会弄乱你的电脑，也碰不到你的个人资料。As Claude works, it runs commands, installs tools and changes files. In a separate space all of that happens inside, so your computer stays tidy and your personal files stay out of reach.

和云电脑相比有什么不同？How is this different from a cloud PC?

云电脑运行在别人的机房，每月通常 ¥250 起，网络一不稳就可能掉线，文件也存在服务商那里。AnyWhere WorkStation 就在你自己的 Mac 上，每月 ¥69，不怕掉线和服务商停服，文件始终在你手里。A cloud PC runs in someone else's data center, usually from $35 a month. A shaky connection can drop you, and your files sit with the provider. AnyWhere WorkStation runs on your own Mac for $10 a month, with no dropouts or provider shutdowns to worry about, and your files always stay with you.

Claude 能看到我的个人文件吗？Can Claude see my personal files?

看不到。Claude 在自己的空间里工作，你的个人文件留在门外。需要它处理某份资料时，由你亲手交给它。No. Claude works in its own space and your personal files stay outside. When you want it to work on something, you hand it over yourself.

价格包含 Claude 订阅吗？Does the price include a Claude subscription?

不包含。Claude 需要你自己的 Anthropic 账号。No. Claude needs your own Anthropic account.

支持哪些 Mac？Which Macs are supported?

搭载 Apple Silicon 芯片、运行 macOS 15 或更新版本的 Mac。Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 15 or later.

月付、年付和永久授权有什么区别？How do monthly, yearly and lifetime differ?

功能完全一样，只是付费方式不同。月付 ¥69；年付 ¥699，比按月付一年省 ¥129；永久授权 ¥999，一次付费，之后不用再续费。Features are exactly the same; only the way you pay differs. Monthly is $10. Yearly is $100, saving $20 over paying monthly. A lifetime license is a one-time $150 with no renewals.

出了问题怎么办？What if something goes wrong?

工作空间可以一键备份，也能一键恢复到之前的状态。Back up your workspace in one click, and restore it to an earlier state just as easily.

订阅到期后，我的资料还在吗？Is my data still there when my subscription ends?

在。工作空间保存在你自己的 Mac 上，到期后你仍然可以取回里面的文件。Yes. Workspaces live on your own Mac, and you can still get your files out after the subscription ends.
